CCSessions privacy policy

CCSessions collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and sends nothing to its developer.

The app connects over SSH only to the server you enter. The server's address, the server's host key and your messages waiting to be sent are kept on your iPhone. The app's SSH key is made on the phone and, where the phone allows, kept in its Secure Enclave, so it can't be copied off it.

Deleting the app deletes everything it kept.

Questions: see the support page.
